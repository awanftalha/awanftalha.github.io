01The problem

HR ran on email, chat and 32 spreadsheets

Across 32 subsidiaries, everyday HR work ran on email, chat and Excel. Every request needed a chaser, every decision was typed in twice, and payroll and finance worked from data that was already out of date.

Leave went by email with HR in copy. Staff were requested by name over chat. New-hire checklists sat with HR, IT and managers separately. Punch exports were retyped for payroll every month.

02Leave requests

Ayesha asks for three days off, and has her answer by lunch

Before, this was an email, two reminders and a sheet. Now Ayesha sees her balance and team cover before she asks, and Bilal decides with everything in one drawer.

  1. 09:12 · Ayesha asks

    Working days are counted, nine left after, and a thin Thursday is flagged before she sends.

  2. 11:40 · Bilal’s inbox

    Oldest first, with balance and cover on every row, so the easy ones go in bulk.

  3. 11:42 · Bilal decides

    Dates, cover and her note in one drawer. Approve is one click; a no needs a reason.

  4. 11:42 · Ayesha knows

    Approved at once. HR and finance already have the record.

03Project resourcing

Mariam needs an engineer, and gets one the same day

Staff used to be requested by name over chat, so the same few people got overbooked. Now Mariam asks for a role, and Omar picks by skills, then by free time.

  1. Mariam spots the gap

    An open role sits in the team table, right where a person should be.

  2. She asks for a role, not a name

    Skills, dates and load, with a live count of how many people match.

  3. Omar finds the best fit

    People ranked by skill match first, then by their free weeks.

  4. He checks the load, then books

    Zain’s weeks after the booking, tentative or confirmed, before anyone is told.

04Onboarding

Hassan signs his offer, and is ready before his first day

Checklists used to sit with HR, IT and managers separately, and new hires waited for laptops. Now one plan starts from the signed offer, and every owner gets their part.

  1. Rabia starts from the offer

    Role, team, manager and start date come straight from the offer. A buddy is suggested.

  2. One plan, every owner

    HR, IT, manager and buddy tasks with due dates, and a readiness score.

  3. Chase only what’s late

    One filter, and one bulk nudge to every owner who is behind.

  4. Hassan’s day one

    A schedule of people to meet, accounts ready and a first-week list.

05Attendance

Ayesha forgets to clock out, and payroll never notices

Punch records used to be retyped for payroll, and missed punches were fixed by email. Now the clock catches the miss, Ayesha fixes it with a request, and Bilal reviews only the exceptions.

  1. Ayesha clocks in

    Her shift, the site and whether she is inside it, before one tap.

  2. A missed clock-out, flagged

    The clock stops at shift end and the day is flagged the next morning.

  3. Bilal sees only exceptions

    Problem days come first. Clean days approve with the week.

  4. Approve, lock, pay

    The week locks on the cut-off and goes to payroll with an audit trail.
