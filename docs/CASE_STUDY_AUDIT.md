## What the audit found

1. **The substance is excellent.** The problem is packaging and consistency. Nothing here needs to be "made more impressive". It needs to be faster to get through.
2. **Three pages are too long for a first read.** The Offer page has 9 chapters, Compliance has 14 and Training has 15. Each runs to roughly 3,000–4,000 words.
3. **There's no motion at all.** There are well over 100 static screenshots and no video, on a portfolio whose strength is interaction.
4. **No page shows the "before".** The old screens and the paper and Excel processes are described in words but never shown.
5. **Two claims conflict with your homepage and testimonials.** Fix these before anything else (see the Offer page and SMS sections).
6. **Every page uses a different skeleton.** The meta fields, chapter names and titles all differ from page to page.
7. **Three pages have a circular "Result" card.** On HX Timer, HX Mobile and SnapGrid, the Result just restates what the product is.
8. **Some wording will go stale.** Phrases like "live for two months", "2 of 3 months done" and "in beta" need an "as of" date.
9. **Your AI-built prototyping appears only once** (Compliance › Validation). Your positioning needs it on more pages.
10. **Correction to my own HOMEPAGE_BRIEF.md.** I labelled NGFT SMS "Shipped" with "cut to hours", but your SMS page says it's a proof of concept that hasn't been built. I also gave the NGFT OPS card a "20 minutes" line that doesn't match the "~50%" in the case study. I'll correct both once you confirm the facts below.

## Proposed formats

| Format | What it is | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| **Case (two-speed)** | A skim layer (about 2 minutes) visible by default, with "Go deeper" sections collapsed below it | Offer page, Compliance, Training, SMS, HX Insight, SnapGrid |
| **App** | A short page: summary, device strip, 3 key moments as video loops, what's next | HX Timer, HX Mobile, Instructor's Tablet |

Training (web) and Instructor's Tablet become **Part 1 / Part 2** of one story. Part 2 stops repeating the research and links back to Part 1 instead.

### The skim layer (the same on every Case page)

Eyebrow → headline → meta (Role · Team · Timeline · Research · Status · Updated) → summary cards (Problem / My role / What changed / Result) → 3–4 stats → **Before / After** → **3 key decisions, each with a 5–15 second loop** → Outcome (the claim and the mechanism behind it) → one line of reflection → CTA and next case study.

**Go deeper** (collapsed): full research, diagnosis tables, reframing, every state, system work, validation, the full reflection, still unresolved, and the full screen set. Nothing is deleted. It's reorganised.

Target: **under 700 words visible** before the first "Go deeper".
