## /* Why it exists */

People filled in the clock sheet at the end of the day or the week. Leaving the work to log time broke focus. And project billing and payroll needed real times. So the timer had to be one button, always in reach, with every entry landing in the clock sheet on its own.

## /* Key moments */

<Decision n="01" title="Switch tasks in one click">
  <Loop slot="media" src="/media/hx-timer/switch.mp4" poster="/assets/work/hx-timer/timer-03.webp" alt="Typing tax in the project picker, choosing a Tax filing portal task, and the timer saving the old entry and starting the new one in the same click." posterAlt="HX Timer tracking 2:41:03 with the project picker open and tax typed: three Tax filing portal tasks, and a note that picking one saves 2h 41m on Payroll revamp and starts the new task at once." device="desktop" />

Picking another task saves the last entry and starts the next one in the same click. The picker says what it's about to save before you choose.
</Decision>

<Decision n="02" title="Ask about idle time, don't guess">
  <Loop slot="media" src="/media/hx-timer/idle.mp4" poster="/assets/work/hx-timer/timer-05.webp" alt="Returning after 14 quiet minutes: the Idle time dialog offers keep, break, move or discard, and the time is discarded." posterAlt="An Idle time dialog over HX Timer: he was away for 14 minutes, with options to keep it on Payroll revamp, call it a break, move it to another project or discard it, and Stop timer or Discard and keep tracking." device="desktop" />

After ten quiet minutes, the app asks what the time was: keep it on the project, call it a break, move it to another project, or discard it. Discarded time never reaches the clock sheet.
</Decision>

<Decision n="03" title="Everything from the menu bar or tray">
  <Loop slot="media" src="/media/hx-timer/menubar.mp4" poster="/assets/work/hx-timer/timer-06.webp" alt="With the window closed, opening the menu bar dropdown, taking a break and switching to another task." posterAlt="The HX Timer menu bar dropdown with the window closed: Payroll revamp running at 1:46:10 with Break and Stop, two tasks to switch to, today at 7h 09m with 55m of breaks, and Add time you forgot." device="desktop" />

Start, stop, break and switch with the window closed. Today's total, the breaks so far and "Add time you forgot" are all in the dropdown.
</Decision>

## /* Where it stands */

Shipped for HX Insight on Windows and Mac. Every entry lands in the HX Insight clock sheet, and the day summary is already filed when the day ends.

All employees now use it to track their time.

The hardest part to get right was idle-time handling.
