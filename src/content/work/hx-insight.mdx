## /* What we found */

I researched with 50 people across the subsidiaries, through interviews, surveys and workshops with HQ HR. Across 32 subsidiaries, everyday HR work ran on email, chat and Excel. Every request needed a chaser, every decision was typed in twice, and payroll and finance worked from data that was already out of date.

Leave, resourcing, onboarding and attendance were the main pain points the research surfaced, so those became the four flows.

## /* Four flows */

</Decision>

## /* How I built it */

I was the lead designer and also a part-time frontend developer on the project. I built the dashboards and the UI for leave management and employee assignment myself.

## /* Outcome */

Shipped in June 2024 and used daily. Every flow flags exceptions and sends only those to HR. Approved leave and locked weeks go straight to payroll and finance, in the columns they already used, which is why the 32 spreadsheets stopped being retyped.

Leaders cut the team halfway through. I kept leading, ran tight sprints and wrote frontend part-time, and it still shipped in six months.
