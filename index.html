Products win or lose in the details. So I design the details.

Three habits from six years of designing and shipping products.

  1. 01

    Diagnose before pixels

    I find out what people are actually trying to do, and where they get stuck, before a single screen changes.

  2. 02

    Design every state

    Empty, loading, error, offline. Each one gets a screen and a way forward, decided before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Design it to ship

    Components, tokens and specs engineers can build without guessing. New patterns only when the work earns them.

Six years in the dense end of software.

I’m Talha. I design the software teams run their work on: quoting, scheduling, compliance reviews, incident handling, the records an audit depends on. Most recently that’s been an operations suite at NGFT, in aviation, a regulated industry where a missed field has real consequences.
